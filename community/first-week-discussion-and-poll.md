# First-Week Community Discussion and Interactive Poll

This page contains ready-to-publish copy for the first community discussion of the week and an accompanying poll. It is designed to welcome beginners, surface member goals, and guide people toward an appropriate first learning activity.

## Discussion post

### Suggested title

**What would you like AI to make easier this week?**

### Ready-to-publish message

Welcome to the first week of the Automation Legends AI Learning Hub.

The most useful way to learn AI is to begin with a real task—not a perfect prompt or a complicated tool. This week, choose one small task that takes time, feels repetitive, or is difficult to begin. Your task could involve learning, writing, planning, organizing, research, design, coding, or a personal project.

> **Your challenge:** Reply with one task you would like AI to make easier this week, and tell us what a good result would look like.

Use this simple format:

```text
My task: [What I want to improve]

A good result would look like: [What “better” means]

My current experience: [new to AI / learning the basics / already using AI]

One boundary or question: [For example: “I need a way to check facts,” or “I cannot share private client information.”]
```

Here is an example:

```text
My task: Turn my weekly notes into a clear plan for the next week.

A good result would look like: A short list of priorities, deadlines, and questions I need to answer.

My current experience: New to AI.

One boundary or question: I want to make sure the AI does not invent deadlines or tasks that were not in my notes.
```

We will respond with a practical next step, a safe starting prompt, or a resource from the hub. You do not need to share confidential information. Use placeholders, fictional examples, or a short description of the task instead.

If you are unsure where to begin, start with the [Beginner Path](../beginner/README.md) and copy the [Prompt Template](../templates/prompt-template.md).

### Moderator response guide

| Member response | Helpful moderator reply |
|---|---|
| Clear beginner task | Celebrate the choice, point to one beginner lesson, and offer a safe first prompt. |
| Vague goal | Ask one clarifying question: “Who is the output for, and what would make it useful?” |
| Sensitive or private context | Thank the member, ask them to remove identifying details, and suggest a fictional or anonymized example. |
| High-stakes request | Encourage qualified human review and frame AI as support for drafting, organizing, or questions—not final decisions. |
| Intermediate workflow | Point the member to the [Workflow Canvas](../templates/workflow-canvas.md) and ask where human review belongs. |

## Interactive poll

### Suggested question

**What is your biggest AI learning priority right now?**

### Poll options

| Option | What the result tells the community |
|---|---|
| **Writing better prompts** | Members need exercises on goals, context, constraints, and output formats. |
| **Learning and research** | Members want explanation, study, source review, and verification practices. |
| **Saving time on routine tasks** | Members are ready to map a workflow and identify a safe AI-assisted step. |
| **Creating content or designs** | Members want help with ideation, drafting, visual creation, and reviewing work. |
| **Understanding AI tools** | Members need a low-pressure tool orientation and one recommended first experiment. |
| **Building automations or projects** | Members may be ready for intermediate workflow design and evaluation. |

### Ready-to-publish poll caption

Vote for the area where you would like the most help this week. We will use the results to choose the next tutorial, office-hour topic, and community challenge.

After voting, add a comment with one sentence that finishes this phrase:

> “If AI could help me with one thing this week, it would be ________.”

There are no wrong answers. We are looking for a useful first experiment, not a complicated project.

## Suggested follow-up after 48 hours

Thank everyone who participated. Share the top poll result, link to the most relevant learning-hub resource, and announce a small challenge tied to that topic.

### Follow-up post template

```text
Thank you to everyone who voted and shared a goal. The top learning priority this week is [poll result].

Start here: [relevant resource link].

Mini-challenge: Spend 15 minutes using one prompt to improve a real, non-sensitive task. Reply with:
1. What you asked AI to do;
2. One part of the output that helped; and
3. One detail you checked or changed yourself.

We will highlight a few useful approaches at the end of the week.
```
