# Week 4 Capstone Launch Email

Use this email to notify opted-in community subscribers about the Week 4 collaborative capstone. Replace every bracketed field before sending. Keep the sender identity, subscription preferences, and unsubscribe information required by your email service provider and applicable requirements.

> **Email principle:** Invite members into a useful learning experience. Do not promise outcomes, imply required participation, or use artificial urgency.

## Ready-to-send email

```text
Hello [First name],

Week 4 of the Automation Legends AI Learning Hub is now open.

This week, we are moving from individual practice to a collaborative capstone: the Community Resource Review Assistant.

Together, we will design a safe workflow that turns an authorized AI-learning resource suggestion into a structured draft for moderator review. The project will not publish content automatically, make final decisions, or use private information. A human moderator remains responsible for every approval.

There is a meaningful role for every member.

Beginner members can help define what makes a resource useful, improve review prompts, test safe examples, create a clear user guide, and document what the team learns.

Intermediate builders can map the workflow, design structured outputs, define the API or no-code automation boundary, build the review queue, test exceptions, and document safe maintenance practices.

The capstone is successful when the team produces a clear, tested, reviewable design. A live deployment is optional and is not required.

Start with the Week 4 capstone module:
https://github.com/Automation-Legends/ai-learning-hub/blob/main/community/week-04-collaborative-capstone.md

Use the feedback and grading rubric to understand the evidence, collaboration, safety, and reflection standards:
https://github.com/Automation-Legends/ai-learning-hub/blob/main/community/week-04-capstone-feedback-and-grading-rubric.md

Your first action:
1. Read the capstone mission and choose a role that fits your current skills.
2. Reply in [the community space] or contact [facilitator name] with the role you would like to try.
3. Bring one question, one learning goal, or one low-risk resource-review idea to the kickoff session on [date and time].

You do not need to be an expert to contribute. Start with what you can test, explain, or improve. The community will build the project together.

[Sender name]
[Organization or sender details]
[Subscription preferences / unsubscribe link]
```

## Send checklist

| Check | Confirm before sending |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | Recipients have opted in to receive this type of community email. |
| **Accurate details** | Kickoff date, time, contact method, role availability, and links are current. |
| **Clear choice** | Members know how to choose a role or ask a question. |
| **Inclusive language** | The email makes clear that technical experience is not required for every role. |
| **Safety scope** | The email accurately states that the project is draft-and-review and does not automatically publish or make final decisions. |
| **Accessibility** | Subject line, link text, and formatting are easy to scan; any accompanying visual has alt text. |
| **Compliance** | Sender identity, required footer content, and subscription controls are included. |
