# AI Community Content Calendar

## A four-week community-growth system for Automation Legends

This calendar provides a practical four-week rhythm for growing an AI community through useful education, member participation, transparent offers, and meaningful recognition. It is designed to help Automation Legends attract learners, create a habit of participation, invite interested members into a paid tier when one exists, and retain members by celebrating progress.

> **Community trust comes first.** Make public content genuinely useful, represent member outcomes accurately, obtain permission before sharing wins or screenshots, and use urgency or scarcity only when it is real and verifiable.

The plan is a starting point rather than a performance guarantee. Test the ideas against your actual audience, capacity, platform rules, and membership offer. Avoid pressuring people to buy; the paid tier should be a clear, optional extension of already valuable free learning.

## The four-part rhythm

Every piece of content has one primary job. The suggested weekly cadence is **4–2–2–1**: four attract posts, two engage posts, two conversion opportunities, and one retention post. A single post may support more than one job, but identifying its main job keeps the community experience balanced.

| Job | Suggested share | Purpose | Example |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Attract** | 40% | Reach new people and establish useful expertise without a paywall | A practical AI-tool review or a clear explanation of an emerging workflow |
| **Engage** | 30% | Build participation, conversation, and peer learning | A poll, challenge, question thread, or live Q&A |
| **Convert** | 20% | Explain the value of an optional paid membership or premium resource | A transparent comparison of free and paid resources |
| **Retain** | 10% | Celebrate members, reinforce belonging, and invite feedback | A member-win roundup or a behind-the-scenes update |

## Ethical operating rules

| Principle | Apply it this way |
|---|---|
| **Value before promotion** | Make free posts complete and useful; a paid offer should add depth, support, templates, or access—not repair a deliberately poor free experience. |
| **Truthful proof** | Use real outcomes, real testimonials, and actual member counts only with permission. Do not invent revenue, time-saved, or success claims. |
| **Genuine urgency** | Use deadlines, limited places, price changes, or founding offers only when they are real, documented, and honored. Never use manufactured scarcity. |
| **Respectful outreach** | Invite highly engaged members to learn more only when the message is personalized, relevant, and easy to decline. Follow each platform’s messaging policies. |
| **Privacy and permission** | Remove private details and obtain permission before sharing member names, images, results, recordings, or screenshots. |
| **Responsible AI claims** | Avoid implying that AI guarantees a business outcome. Teach members to test outputs, verify important claims, and retain human judgment. |

## Week 1 — Authority Build

**Goal:** Establish useful expertise and make new members confident that Automation Legends offers practical, grounded AI guidance. Keep promotion light and focus on service.

| Day | Job | Content piece | Format and execution | Optional membership invitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Attract | **Three AI tools that saved time this week** | Create a short carousel, thread, or video showing a real, reproducible workflow. Explain the task, the tool’s role, and the human review step. | Offer a link to a deeper prompt breakdown or workflow template if one exists. |
| Tuesday | Engage | **What is your biggest AI bottleneck?** | Run a poll with options such as prompting, integrations, strategy, cost, or evaluation. Reply to useful comments with a specific tip. | Invite engaged members to a relevant guide or trial without pressure. |
| Wednesday | Attract | **AI news that matters** | Curate up to three updates and explain the practical impact in plain language. Keep the post concise and distinguish fact from opinion. | Add a clearly labeled preview of a longer member analysis, if available. |
| Thursday | Engage | **Build something with AI in 30 minutes** | Publish a safe starter prompt and ask members to share a sanitized result. Offer constructive feedback and celebrate first attempts. | Explain that paid members may receive an advanced version, office hours, or feedback only if those benefits are actually available. |
| Friday | Convert | **A workflow or prompt case study** | Share a before-and-after process with honest constraints, the human review step, and a reusable excerpt. | Offer the full template, detailed walkthrough, or resource vault through a direct but transparent call to action. |
| Saturday | Engage | **Ask Me Anything** | Host a one-hour text, audio, or video session. Answer questions generously and collect themes for future content. | Mention ongoing office hours only if they are scheduled and accessible to members. |
| Sunday | Retain | **Wins of the week** | Celebrate projects, lessons, and helpful contributions. Get permission before tagging members or sharing screenshots. | Briefly preview the next week’s member activities or resources. |

## Week 2 — Participation and Discovery

**Goal:** Help free members see the quality of inside conversations, templates, feedback, and practical support without creating an artificial divide.

| Day | Job | Content piece | Format and execution | Optional membership invitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Attract | **How an organization improved a process with AI** | Use a public, verifiable case study or a clearly anonymized internal example. Explain the workflow, trade-offs, and limits. | Link to an approved implementation checklist or deeper template. |
| Tuesday | Convert | **Inside the resource library** | Show a truthful preview of available templates, playbooks, workshops, or recordings. List what each resource does and who it helps. | State the exact membership price, access terms, and cancellation policy. |
| Wednesday | Engage | **A constructive hot take** | Publish a specific, debatable claim such as “Most AI workflows fail because success criteria are undefined.” Ask members to explain their experience. | Share a related deep-dive resource with people who ask for it. |
| Thursday | Attract | **One tool, three use cases** | Show three practical applications of one tool, each with a safe prompt and a review step. | Offer an advanced workflow guide or downloadable checklist with clear access terms. |
| Friday | Convert | **Member spotlight** | Interview a member about the problem they tackled, the process they used, the results they can substantiate, and what remained difficult. | Let the member describe the value in their own words; never script false claims. |
| Saturday | Engage | **Share your project for one AI idea** | Invite members to post a short project description. Give one actionable, bounded suggestion to each participant as capacity permits. | If deeper weekly feedback exists, invite members to it respectfully. |
| Sunday | Retain | **What we are building** | Share an honest behind-the-scenes update on a new resource, event, or feature. Invite feedback before finalizing it. | Explain any beta-testing opportunity and what members can expect. |

## Week 3 — Proof and Practical Demonstration

**Goal:** Address common objections by showing what the community offers, how members use it, and what thoughtful AI practice looks like.

| Day | Job | Content piece | Format and execution | Optional membership invitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Convert | **Resource-library tour** | Record a concise walkthrough of the actual resource library. Show navigation, examples, and the type of help members receive. | End with access details and any genuine enrollment window. |
| Tuesday | Attract | **What is real, next, and overhyped in AI** | Share a balanced forecast with clear reasoning. Distinguish prediction from current fact and invite respectful disagreement. | Link to a longer forecast or discussion resource. |
| Wednesday | Engage | **Share your AI stack** | List the tools, purpose, price range, and what you stopped using. Include limitations and avoid unsupported “replaced employees” claims. | Mention any member-built tool or template only if it is actually accessible through membership. |
| Thursday | Convert | **Free and paid resources this month** | Make a visual comparison that accurately lists free resources, paid resources, events, feedback, and support. | Make the value proposition specific and easy to understand. |
| Friday | Attract | **One prompt that improved a real workflow** | Give away a high-quality, broadly useful prompt and explain how to adapt and verify it. | Offer a related prompt collection if it exists. |
| Saturday | Engage | **Build an AI workflow live** | Teach one concrete workflow from start to finish, including data boundaries, quality checks, and human review. | At the end, offer the full framework or additional builds without interrupting the lesson. |
| Sunday | Retain | **Thank you and what is coming next** | Thank members, recognize contributors, and preview confirmed upcoming content. | Give paid members a clear, factual preview of next month’s benefits. |

## Week 4 — Clear Invitation and Retention

**Goal:** Reduce friction for members who are already interested while staying transparent about pricing, access, and expectations.

| Day | Job | Content piece | Format and execution | Optional membership invitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Convert | **Upcoming membership change** | If a price or access change is real, explain what changes, why it changes, and when it takes effect. | Offer a genuine founding or grandfather option only when it will be honored. |
| Tuesday | Engage | **What would you build with a prompt library?** | Ask members to describe a workflow they would like to create. Reply with practical starter ideas. | Share a relevant template or describe how paid members receive deeper support. |
| Wednesday | Convert | **Member stories and testimonials** | Gather permissioned stories in a carousel, thread, or video. Include context, not only impressive outcomes. | Offer a clear trial, refund, or cancellation policy if one exists. |
| Thursday | Attract | **The workflow I wish I knew earlier** | Share the month’s strongest free tutorial. Include steps, limitations, and a usable template. | Add a modest footer invitation to the related premium playbook. |
| Friday | Convert | **Enrollment reminder** | Publish a short reminder only when a real deadline, capacity limit, or price change applies. | State the exact deadline and what happens after it. |
| Saturday | Retain | **Welcome new members and community shoutouts** | Celebrate participation and introduce newcomers in a way they approve. | Invite interested free members to register for the next onboarding or event. |
| Sunday | Retain | **Monthly retrospective** | Share what worked, what did not, what the community learned, and what will change next month. | Explain how member feedback shaped the next plan. |

## Nine responsible conversion practices

| Practice | Use it responsibly |
|---|---|
| **Resource preview** | Show a real excerpt or a partial template while ensuring the free version remains independently useful. |
| **Member-only preview box** | In a newsletter, mention recently published member resources with a direct access link and clear description. |
| **Member spotlight** | Let a consenting member explain their process and outcome in their own words. |
| **Workshop invitation** | Teach a complete free lesson, then offer an optional next step after the teaching is complete. |
| **Personal outreach** | Message engaged people only with relevant, permission-based invitations that are easy to decline. |
| **Genuine scarcity** | Use time limits, access limits, or price changes only when they are real and documented. |
| **Free-versus-paid comparison** | Show concrete resource, support, event, and access differences without disparaging free members. |
| **Risk reversal** | State clear, fair trial, refund, and cancellation terms—and follow them. |
| **Founding offer** | Offer an early-member rate only if the eligibility and duration are stated plainly and honored consistently. |

## Ten reusable post templates

### 1. Tested-tool review

> “I spent time testing **[tool]** for **[specific task]**. Here is what worked, what did not, and who should consider it. The key lesson was **[specific insight]**.”

Follow with a transparent link to a decision guide, prompt, or workflow template if you have one.

### 2. Before-and-after workflow

> “Before AI, **[manual process]** required **[time or effort]**. With a carefully reviewed AI step, the process now produces **[specific output]**. Here are the three steps and the guardrail that matters most.”

Share a complete, useful version of the workflow. Offer an optional advanced template or walkthrough as the next step.

### 3. Constructive hot take

> “A common AI mistake is **[specific behavior]**. I think it fails because **[reason]**. Here is a better approach: **[practical alternative]**. What has your experience been?”

### 4. Share-a-prompt post

> “This prompt helped me **[task]**. Copy it, adapt the bracketed fields, and verify the output before using it: **[sanitized prompt]**.”

Link to a complete prompt collection only if it is genuinely available.

### 5. Newsletter preview

> “This week: **[free practical lesson]**. Member resource preview: **[one-sentence factual description]**. Read the free lesson first, then choose the deeper resource if it fits your needs.”

### 6. Community challenge

> “Challenge: use this safe starter prompt to **[task]**. Share a sanitized result or what you learned. I will review a selection of responses and highlight strong approaches.”

### 7. Tool-stack reveal

> “My current AI stack: **[tool]** for **[purpose]**, **[tool]** for **[purpose]**, and **[tool]** for **[purpose]**. Here is what I pay, what I no longer use, and the trade-off behind each choice.”

### 8. Forecast post

> “Three AI developments I am watching in **[industry]**: **[trend 1]**, **[trend 2]**, and **[trend 3]**. These are predictions, not guarantees. Here is the evidence and uncertainty behind each.”

### 9. Office-hours invitation

> “I am hosting an AI office hour on **[date and time]**. Bring your prompt, workflow, or question. We will focus on practical next steps and safe implementation.”

### 10. Wins-of-the-week celebration

> “This week, Automation Legends members **[permissioned, accurate wins]**. Thank you to everyone who shared their learning, asked thoughtful questions, and helped a peer.”

| Publish two attract pieces that teach a practical skill or explain a relevant development | ☐ |
| Publish two engage pieces such as a poll, challenge, Q&A, or discussion prompt | ☐ |
| Publish one clear, accurate conversion invitation when a membership or premium resource exists | ☐ |
| Publish one retention piece that celebrates members or gathers feedback | ☐ |
| Send one newsletter with an actionable free lesson and a clearly labeled resource preview | ☐ |
| Invite a small number of genuinely engaged members to a relevant next step, only when appropriate | ☐ |
| Repurpose one deeper resource into a valuable free teaser without misleading omissions | ☐ |
| Request permissioned feedback, testimonials, or win stories | ☐ |

## Metrics to review weekly

The following measures help you learn what works for your community. They are diagnostic signals, not promises or universal targets. Track trends over time and pair numbers with qualitative member feedback.

| Measure | Definition | What it can tell you |
|---|---|---|
| **Free-to-paid conversion** | New paid members divided by eligible free members or visitors, using a consistent definition | Whether the paid offer and invitation are clear and relevant |
| **Engagement** | Meaningful replies, discussion participation, event attendance, and direct questions | Whether content is useful enough to prompt conversation |
| **Newsletter performance** | Delivery, open, click, reply, and conversion activity where privacy rules permit measurement | Whether the subject, content, and next step resonate |
| **Retention and churn** | Continuing memberships, cancellations, reasons given, and renewal behavior | Whether the paid experience keeps delivering value |
| **Content-to-outcome link** | Trials, sign-ups, event registrations, or helpful discussions tied to a specific post | Which topics and formats deserve more investment |
| **Member-sentiment signals** | Survey responses, support questions, qualitative feedback, and concerns | Whether the community feels helpful, safe, and respectful |

## Monthly review questions

At the end of each four-week cycle, review the evidence with your team or community moderators.

1. Which content created the most useful discussion, not only the most clicks?
2. Which topics led to members completing a project, learning a skill, or helping one another?
3. Which invitation was clearest and most respectful?
4. Did any content claim more than the evidence supported?
5. Where did members encounter friction, confusion, or an unmet expectation?
6. Which resources should be improved, expanded, or retired next month?

## Final principle

Free content should stand on its own as useful education. Premium content should provide a natural next level of depth, support, community access, or reusable tools. When members can see the value clearly and trust the community’s claims, growth becomes an outcome of service rather than pressure.
