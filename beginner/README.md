# Beginner Path

The Beginner Path is for members who want to use generative AI thoughtfully and effectively, even if they have never written a prompt before. The goal is not to memorize one tool. It is to develop transferable habits: define the task clearly, provide useful context, request a usable format, and independently review anything important.

> **Recommended mindset:** Let AI accelerate drafts and analysis, but keep human judgment over facts, decisions, and final work.

## Learning sequence

## What you need

You only need access to an AI assistant of your choice, a place to save notes, and a small task you are comfortable improving. Use fictional or non-sensitive examples when practicing. Do not paste private customer data, credentials, medical information, legal documents, or employer-confidential material into an AI tool unless you have explicit authorization and understand the tool’s data-handling terms.

## You are ready for Intermediate when…

You can reliably explain the result you want, ask for an output in a specific format, improve a response by adding useful context, identify when an answer needs checking, and finish a small AI-assisted task without relying on the AI as the final authority.

## Quick-start activity

Choose one small task that repeats each week, such as drafting a meeting agenda, organizing study notes, creating a social-media content outline, or turning a long article into questions. Write down the current process in three sentences. You will improve this task across the four modules.

## Completion standard

Complete the activity and reflection in all four modules. Then use the [project showcase template](../templates/project-showcase.md) to describe what you tried, what improved, what still needed human review, and what you would change next time.
